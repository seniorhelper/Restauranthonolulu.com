    Bakeries & malasadas in Honolulu

    Malasadas, coco puffs, butter mochi and pastries.

    Leonard's Bakery

    Bakeries & malasadas · Shave ice & dessert

    Kapahulu Portuguese bakery making malasadas since 1952; plain sugar and filled malasada puffs.

    Liliha$

    Liliha Bakery Kuakini

    Bakeries & malasadas · Breakfast & brunch · American

    Original 1950 Liliha Bakery with its old-school counter diner; known for coco puffs.

    Waikīkī$$

    Liliha Bakery Waikīkī Beach Walk

    Bakeries & malasadas · Breakfast & brunch · American

    Liliha Bakery's sixth Oʻahu location, opened July 2026 on the second level of Waikīkī Beach Walk.

    Open late
    Kalihi$

    Nisshodo Candy Store

    Shave ice & dessert · Japanese · Bakeries & malasadas

    Century-old Kalihi mochi shop making traditional Japanese sweets; chichi dango often sells out daily.

    Waikīkī—

    Hala Tree Café Waikīkī

    Coffee · Bakeries & malasadas

    Flagship café of the Hala Tree Kona coffee farm, moved from Kaʻaʻawa to The Lilia Waikīkī in June 2026. It pours the farm’s own 100% Kona coffee and has an in-house bakery; the entrance is on Kānekapōlei Street.

    Kalihi$

    Kamehameha Bakery

    Bakeries & malasadas

    Pre-dawn Kalihi bakery known for purple poi-glazed donuts and malasadas; popular items sell out later in the day.

    Airport & Iwilei$$

    Liliha Bakery Nimitz

    Bakeries & malasadas · Breakfast & brunch · American

    Larger sit-down Liliha Bakery on Nimitz Highway serving all-day diner food and coco puffs.

    North Shore—

    Ted's Bakery

    Bakeries & malasadas

    Family-run bakery near Sunset Beach on the North Shore, best known for chocolate haupia pie. It stopped wholesale distribution in 2022 and sells its pies and cakes at the shop.
