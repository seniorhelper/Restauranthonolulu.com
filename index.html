      Quick answers

      Where should a first-time visitor eat Hawaiian food?

      Helena's Hawaiian Food in Kalihi (James Beard America’s Classic) or Highway Inn Kakaʻako. Order kalua pig, laulau, lomi salmon and poi.

      Where is the best poke near Waikīkī?

      Ono Seafood on Kapahulu Ave is a short ride from Waikīkī. In Chinatown, Maguro Brothers Chinatown. See all poke.

      Do I need reservations?

      For La Mer, Orchids, Senia and sunset tables, yes — often a week or more ahead. Plate lunch, poke and shave ice are walk-in. See the reservations guide.
