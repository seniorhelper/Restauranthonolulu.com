Questions people actually ask

What are the best restaurants in Honolulu right now?

Helena's Hawaiian Food in Kalihi for Hawaiian food. House Without a Key for a Waikiki sunset. Senia in Chinatown for a chef's counter.

What are the best restaurants in Waikiki?

Oceanfront for the view, hotel dining that's actually good, and walkable rooms for breakfast and poke.

Do I need a reservation at Honolulu restaurants?

Yes for tasting menus and famous sunset rooms — often a week out. No for plate lunch, poke, food trucks.

Is Honolulu expensive to eat in?

Plate lunch or a food truck is still about $12–20. Casual sit-down $25–50 before drinks.

What Hawaiian food should I try in Honolulu?

Plate lunch, poke, kalua pig, lomi salmon, and poi. Helena's is the classic classroom.

Where do locals eat in Honolulu?

Kaimuki, Kapahulu, Kalihi, Chinatown, and Kakaako more than the Waikiki strip.
