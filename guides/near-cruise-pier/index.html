    Guide

    Near the cruise pier

    Restaurants within walking distance or a short ride of the Aloha Tower cruise terminal and Pier 2.

    Downtown$$$

    Bar Leather Apron

    Cocktail bars

    Downtown cocktail bar that won the 2023 James Beard Award for Outstanding Bar; reservations recommended.

    0.3 mi from Aloha Tower cruise terminal

    James Beard Outstanding Bar 2023
    Chinatown$$$

    Fête

    American · Farm-to-table

    Chinatown New American restaurant from chef Robynne Maiʻi, 2022 James Beard Best Chef: Northwest & Pacific.

    0.4 mi from Aloha Tower cruise terminal

    James Beard Best Chef 2022
    Chinatown$$$$

    Senia

    Fine dining · Fusion

    Chef Anthony Rush's Chinatown restaurant: à la carte dining room plus an 8-seat Fri-Sat chef's counter tasting menu.

    0.4 mi from Aloha Tower cruise terminal

    Chinatown$$

    Bar 35

    Bars & pubs · Pizza

    Downtown/Chinatown bar on Hotel Street with indoor-outdoor space, pizzas, a big beer list and weekend DJs.

    0.4 mi from Aloha Tower cruise terminal

    Chinatown$

    Maguro Brothers Chinatown

    Poke · Seafood · Japanese

    Fishmonger-run poke and sashimi counter inside Kekaulike Market; daytime only and often sells out by noon.

    0.4 mi from Aloha Tower cruise terminal

    Chinatown$$

    Lucky Belly

    Ramen · Fusion · Bars & pubs

    Chinatown Asian-fusion noodle bar on Hotel Street serving ramen bowls and small plates into the late evening.

    0.4 mi from Aloha Tower cruise terminal

    Open late
    Chinatown$$$

    Livestock Tavern

    American · Bars & pubs

    Chinatown tavern serving meat-centric American dinners Tuesday-Sunday plus weekend brunch.

    0.4 mi from Aloha Tower cruise terminal

    Open late
    Chinatown$$

    Legend Seafood Restaurant

    Dim sum · Chinese · Seafood

    Cantonese dim sum and seafood hall in Chinatown Cultural Plaza; weekend tables fill early with families.

    0.5 mi from Aloha Tower cruise terminal

    Chinatown$

    Lam's Kitchen

    Chinese · Udon & noodles

    Chinatown Cantonese noodle shop for wonton min and roast meats; renovated and expanded, reopening Dec 2025.

    0.6 mi from Aloha Tower cruise terminal

    Kakaʻako$$

    Aloha Beer Co.

    Breweries · Bars & pubs · American

    Kakaʻako brewery with taproom and open-air beer garden; Wednesday trivia, Saturday live music.

    0.7 mi from Aloha Tower cruise terminal

    Happy hourOpen late
    Kakaʻako$$

    Mad Pie

    Pizza

    James Orlando's brick-and-mortar follow-up to the Fatto a Mano market pop-up; sourdough pies at Keauhou Lane.

    0.8 mi from Aloha Tower cruise terminal

    Kakaʻako$$

    Moku Kitchen

    Hawaiʻi Regional · Pizza · Bars & pubs

    Peter Merriman's casual Kakaʻako restaurant in SALT at Our Kakaʻako; known for a busy afternoon happy hour.

    0.8 mi from Aloha Tower cruise terminal

    Kakaʻako$$

    Highway Inn Kakaʻako

    Hawaiian food · Plate lunch & local food

    Kakaʻako branch of the Waipahu Hawaiian-food restaurant founded in 1947; serves laulau, kalua pig and plate lunches.

    0.8 mi from Aloha Tower cruise terminal

    Hale ʻAina 2026 Silver
    Kakaʻako$$$$

    53 By The Sea

    Fine dining · Pacific Rim

    Kakaʻako waterfront restaurant with Diamond Head views; dinner Wed-Sun, Sunday brunch, business-casual dress.

    0.8 mi from Aloha Tower cruise terminal

    Ocean view
    Kakaʻako$$$

    MW Restaurant

    Hawaiʻi Regional

    Hawaiʻi regional restaurant from Wade Ueoka and Michelle Karr-Ueoka, now on the 2nd floor of the Velocity Honolulu building.

    0.9 mi from Aloha Tower cruise terminal

    Airport & Iwilei$$

    Nico's Pier 38

    Seafood · Plate lunch & local food

    Harborside Pier 38 seafood restaurant and fish market; nightly live music listed through 2026.

    1.0 mi from Aloha Tower cruise terminal

    Nuʻuanu$$

    Chao

    Vietnamese

    Nuʻuanu Ave Vietnamese restaurant; 2026 Hale ʻAina Best New Restaurant and Best Vietnamese.

    1.1 mi from Aloha Tower cruise terminal

    Hale ʻAina 2026
    Ward Village$$$

    Merriman's Honolulu

    Hawaiʻi Regional · Farm-to-table

    Peter Merriman's Ward Village farm-to-table restaurant; Merriman was a 2026 James Beard Outstanding Restaurateur semifinalist.

    1.2 mi from Aloha Tower cruise terminal

    James Beard semifinalist 2026
    Kalihi$

    Nisshodo Candy Store

    Shave ice & dessert · Japanese · Bakeries & malasadas

    Century-old Kalihi mochi shop making traditional Japanese sweets; chichi dango often sells out daily.

    1.2 mi from Aloha Tower cruise terminal

    Liliha$

    Liliha Bakery Kuakini

    Bakeries & malasadas · Breakfast & brunch · American

    Original 1950 Liliha Bakery with its old-school counter diner; known for coco puffs.

    1.2 mi from Aloha Tower cruise terminal

    Kakaʻako$$$

    Istanbul

    Turkish · Mediterranean

    Turkish restaurant in Ward Village from mother-daughter chefs Nili Yildirim and Ahu Hettema; mezze and pide.

    1.2 mi from Aloha Tower cruise terminal

    Ward Village$$

    Westman Café

    Breakfast & brunch · Japanese

    Brunch café relocated from Beach Walk to the Kōʻula building in Ward Village; added dinner service.

    1.2 mi from Aloha Tower cruise terminal

    Kakaʻako$$$$

    Yakitori Torishin

    Japanese · Fine dining

    Honolulu outpost of the formerly Michelin-starred NYC yakitori bar; omakase-only at Aeʻo, Ward Village.

    1.3 mi from Aloha Tower cruise terminal

    Kalihi$$$

    Yohei Sushi

    Sushi · Japanese

    Traditional sushi bar in Kalihi's Kokea Center strip mall; 2026 Hale ʻAina Best Overall Japanese Gold.

    1.4 mi from Aloha Tower cruise terminal

    Hale ʻAina 2026 Gold
    Kalihi$

    Alicia's Market

    Poke · Plate lunch & local food

    Family-run Kalihi market and deli back in its original Mokauea St spot after a fire; known for poke and roast meats.

    1.5 mi from Aloha Tower cruise terminal

    Kalihi$

    Kamehameha Bakery

    Bakeries & malasadas

    Pre-dawn Kalihi bakery known for purple poi-glazed donuts and malasadas; popular items sell out later in the day.

    1.5 mi from Aloha Tower cruise terminal

    Ala Moana$$

    Side Street Inn (Hopaka)

    Plate lunch & local food · Bars & pubs

    Original Side Street Inn near Ala Moana, a pau hana spot for family-style local plates.

    1.5 mi from Aloha Tower cruise terminal

    Distances are straight-line from Aloha Tower cruise terminal (Piers 10/11, with Pier 2 nearby). Restaurant coordinates are approximate (about 100 m), and places without coordinates are not included.

    Good to know

    • Chinatown is about a 10-minute walk from Aloha Tower; Kakaʻako is a 5-minute ride.
    • Downtown lunch counters often close by 2 pm and on weekends — check hours on each page.
