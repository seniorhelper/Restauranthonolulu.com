    Guide

    Ala Moana Center

    Restaurants in and within a mile of Ala Moana Center, the walkable area between Waikīkī and Kakaʻako.

    Ala Moana$$$

    Jade Dynasty Seafood Restaurant

    Chinese · Dim sum · Seafood

    Hong Kong-style seafood and dim sum restaurant on Ala Moana Center's Hoʻokipa Terrace.

    0.0 mi from Ala Moana Center

    Hale ʻAina 2026 Gold
    Ala Moana$$$$

    The Signature Prime Steak & Seafood

    Steakhouses · Seafood · Fine dining

    Steakhouse on the 36th floor of the Ala Moana Hotel next to Ala Moana Center; nightly happy hour with half-off pupu reported.

    0.2 mi from Ala Moana Center

    Ala Moana$$

    Side Street Inn (Hopaka)

    Plate lunch & local food · Bars & pubs

    Original Side Street Inn near Ala Moana, a pau hana spot for family-style local plates.

    0.3 mi from Ala Moana Center

    Kakaʻako$$$$

    Yakitori Torishin

    Japanese · Fine dining

    Honolulu outpost of the formerly Michelin-starred NYC yakitori bar; omakase-only at Aeʻo, Ward Village.

    0.6 mi from Ala Moana Center

    Makiki$$

    Kevin's Kitchen

    Chinese

    Cantonese restaurant and roast-meat counter on S. Beretania, in the Times Supermarket plaza; BYOB, banquet menus.

    0.6 mi from Ala Moana Center

    Mōʻiliʻili$

    Yama's Fish Market

    Hawaiian food · Poke · Plate lunch & local food

    Takeout-only Mōʻiliʻili fish market and deli for Hawaiian plates, poke and haupia pie.

    0.6 mi from Ala Moana Center

    McCully$

    Waiola Shave Ice (Waiola St)

    Shave ice & dessert

    Original Waiola shave ice shop on Waiola St with fine-shaved ice and old-school toppings.

    0.7 mi from Ala Moana Center

    Kakaʻako$$$

    Istanbul

    Turkish · Mediterranean

    Turkish restaurant in Ward Village from mother-daughter chefs Nili Yildirim and Ahu Hettema; mezze and pide.

    0.7 mi from Ala Moana Center

    Ward Village$$

    Westman Café

    Breakfast & brunch · Japanese

    Brunch café relocated from Beach Walk to the Kōʻula building in Ward Village; added dinner service.

    0.7 mi from Ala Moana Center

    Ward Village$$$

    Merriman's Honolulu

    Hawaiʻi Regional · Farm-to-table

    Peter Merriman's Ward Village farm-to-table restaurant; Merriman was a 2026 James Beard Outstanding Restaurateur semifinalist.

    0.8 mi from Ala Moana Center

    James Beard semifinalist 2026
    Kakaʻako$$$

    MW Restaurant

    Hawaiʻi Regional

    Hawaiʻi regional restaurant from Wade Ueoka and Michelle Karr-Ueoka, now on the 2nd floor of the Velocity Honolulu building.

    1.0 mi from Ala Moana Center

    Waikīkī$$$

    Monkeypod Kitchen Waikīkī

    American · Pizza · Bars & pubs

    Peter Merriman's Monkeypod at the Outrigger Reef; three happy hours daily including late night added in 2026.

    1.0 mi from Ala Moana Center

    James Beard semifinalist 2026Happy hourOpen late
