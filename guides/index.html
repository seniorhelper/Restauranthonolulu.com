How to use this guide

Don't try to eat Honolulu. Eat three good meals a day and one special night. Start with a craving — poke, plate lunch, sunset, a chef's counter — then pick a neighborhood that is good at that thing. Waikiki is convenient. It is not the whole city.

A weekend in four meals

Breakfast on the strip. Poke or a plate for lunch. Sunset if you want the postcard. Town (Kakaako, Chinatown, Kaimuki) for the dinner you'll remember. That's it. The rest is weather and mood.
