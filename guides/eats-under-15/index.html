    Eats under $15

    Places where a full meal is about $15 or less per person.

    Own a restaurant under $15? Put it at the top of this page and in matching searches. Pop-up Domination · Claim your free listing

    North Shore$

    Matsumoto Shave Ice

    Shave ice & dessert

    Haleʻiwa shave ice shop founded in 1951; marked 75 years in business in 2026.

    Under $15 per person

    Kapahulu$

    Leonard's Bakery

    Bakeries & malasadas · Shave ice & dessert

    Kapahulu Portuguese bakery making malasadas since 1952; plain sugar and filled malasada puffs.

    Under $15 per person

    Liliha$

    Liliha Bakery Kuakini

    Bakeries & malasadas · Breakfast & brunch · American

    Original 1950 Liliha Bakery with its old-school counter diner; known for coco puffs.

    Under $15 per person

    Kapahulu$

    Rainbow Drive-In Kapahulu

    Plate lunch & local food

    Original Rainbow Drive-In plate-lunch stand, open since 1961 on Kanaina Ave near Kapahulu; known for mix plates and loco moco.

    Under $15 per person

    Waikīkī$

    Musubi Cafe Iyasume

    Japanese · Plate lunch & local food

    Takeout musubi and bento shop on Kūhiō Ave at the Aqua Pacific Monarch; part of a small Honolulu chain.

    Under $15 per person

    Kalihi$

    Nisshodo Candy Store

    Shave ice & dessert · Japanese · Bakeries & malasadas

    Century-old Kalihi mochi shop making traditional Japanese sweets; chichi dango often sells out daily.

    Under $15 per person

    ʻAiea & Pearl City$

    Forty Niner Restaurant

    Plate lunch & local food · Udon & noodles

    Old-time ʻAiea diner on the Hawaiʻi historic register, best known for inexpensive saimin; short drive from Pearl Harbor.

    Under $15 per person

    Kalihi$

    Alicia's Market

    Poke · Plate lunch & local food

    Family-run Kalihi market and deli back in its original Mokauea St spot after a fire; known for poke and roast meats.

    Under $15 per person

    Kalihi$

    Kamehameha Bakery

    Bakeries & malasadas

    Pre-dawn Kalihi bakery known for purple poi-glazed donuts and malasadas; popular items sell out later in the day.

    Under $15 per person

    North Shore$

    Kono's

    Breakfast & brunch · American

    Haleʻiwa counter known for 12-hour kālua pork and Breakfast Bomber burritos.

    Under $15 per person

    Chinatown$

    Lam's Kitchen

    Chinese · Udon & noodles

    Chinatown Cantonese noodle shop for wonton min and roast meats; renovated and expanded, reopening Dec 2025.

    Under $15 per person

    Chinatown$

    Maguro Brothers Chinatown

    Poke · Seafood · Japanese

    Fishmonger-run poke and sashimi counter inside Kekaulike Market; daytime only and often sells out by noon.

    Under $15 per person

    Waikīkī$

    Marugame Udon Waikīkī

    Udon & noodles · Japanese

    Cafeteria-style Kuhio Ave udon shop with noodles made in view and a la carte tempura; lines are common.

    Under $15 per person

    Kapahulu$

    Ono Seafood

    Poke · Seafood

    Small Kapahulu poke counter known for made-to-order shoyu and spicy ahi bowls; often sells out.

    Under $15 per person

    McCully$

    Waiola Shave Ice (Waiola St)

    Shave ice & dessert

    Original Waiola shave ice shop on Waiola St with fine-shaved ice and old-school toppings.

    Under $15 per person

    Kapahulu$

    Waiola Shave Ice Kapahulu

    Shave ice & dessert

    Kapahulu branch of Waiola Shave Ice on Mokihana St, off Kapahulu Ave.

    Under $15 per person

    Mōʻiliʻili$

    Yama's Fish Market

    Hawaiian food · Poke · Plate lunch & local food

    Takeout-only Mōʻiliʻili fish market and deli for Hawaiian plates, poke and haupia pie.

    Under $15 per person

    Kaimukī$

    Fresh Catch Kaimukī

    Poke · Seafood · Plate lunch & local food

    Waiʻalae Ave poke and seafood counter with a few tables; sister shop in Kāneʻohe.

    Under $15 per person

    Kāneʻohe$

    Fresh Catch Kāneʻohe

    Poke · Seafood

    Kāneʻohe poke shop and fish market, the larger of Fresh Catch's two locations.

    Under $15 per person

    Waikīkī$

    Jalapeños 808

    Mexican & tacos

    Mexican counter's first Waikīkī location, in the Royal Hawaiian Center food court.

    Under $15 per person

    Kalihi$

    Rainbow Drive-In Kalihi

    Plate lunch & local food

    Second Rainbow Drive-In, opened 2018 at School and Houghtailing streets with the Kapahulu menu.

    Under $15 per person

    Ala Moana$

    Tandoori Express

    Indian

    Pakistani and North Indian counter at Ala Moana Center.

    Under $15 per person

    Good to know

    • Plate lunch, poke by the pound, musubi and bakery runs are the budget staples.
    • Prices change often; the $ rating means under about $15 per person.
