$$ · Kalihi

Helena's Hawaiian Food

Traditional Hawaiian

James Beard America's Classic, open since 1946. Pipikaula, butterfish collar, poi. Cash, a line, and the taste that explains Hawaiian food better than any tasting menu.

$$$ · Waikiki

House Without a Key

Waikiki sunset dining

Live hula, Diamond Head, and a coconut cake people fly home talking about. Not the most ambitious kitchen on the island. Possibly the most Honolulu evening you can buy.

$$$ · Kaimuki

Mud Hen Water

Modern Hawaiian

Ed Kenney's Kaimuki room is the recommendation locals give visitors who are ready to leave the strip. Farm-serious, bar-serious, and deeply Oahu.

$$$$ · Chinatown

Senia

Pacific tasting menu

Chinatown's most coveted chef's counter. Small plates and tasting menus that treat Hawaiian product with the same attention as any coastal city that already has Michelin.

$$ · Pier 38

Nico's Pier 38

Fish market lunch

The working-waterfront answer to 'where should we eat fish?' Plate lunches, ahi, and a harbor you can smell. Come for lunch. Don't dress up.

$$$ · Ward Village

Merriman's Honolulu

Hawaii Regional Cuisine

Peter Merriman helped invent farm-to-table in these islands. The Ward Village room is the polished version — local fish, island beef, a lunch worth planning around.

Thai Tacos Waikiki

Paid partner. Fusion tacos with real Thai heat, parked in Waikiki. If you want street food that isn't a plate-lunch clone, start here.
