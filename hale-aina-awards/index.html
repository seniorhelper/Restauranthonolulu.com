    Hale ʻAina Awards

    Results of Honolulu Magazine’s annual Hale ʻAina restaurant awards, voted by readers. Winners link to their pages here.

    2026 winners

    Announced June 9, 2026. Reader-voted, except Restaurateur of the Year. Selected categories shown.

    Best New Restaurant

    1. Gold Chao Hawaiʻi
    2. Silver Botanico Waikīkī
    3. Bronze Earls Waikīkī
    4. Finalist ROKC Hawaiʻi

    Best Oʻahu Restaurant

    1. Gold Giovedì
    2. Silver Arancino at The Kāhala
    3. Bronze Kapa Hale
    4. Finalist Yohei Sushi

    Best Maui Restaurant

    1. Gold Mala Ocean Tavern
    2. Silver Mama's Fish House
    3. Bronze Merriman's Kapalua
    4. Finalist Monkeypod Kitchen Kāʻanapali

    Best Hawaiʻi Island Restaurant

    1. Gold Merriman's Waimea
    2. Silver Moon and Turtle
    3. Bronze Engawa by Islander Sake Brewery
    4. Finalist Kaleo's Bar & Grill

    Best Kauaʻi Restaurant

    1. Gold ʻĀina Kauaʻi Restaurant
    2. Silver The Beach House
    3. Bronze Merriman's Kauaʻi
    4. Finalist RumFire Poʻipū Beach

    Best Overall Japanese

    1. Gold Yohei Sushi
    2. Silver Zigu
    3. Bronze Restaurant I-naba

    Best Tonkatsu

    1. Gold Tonkatsu Tamafuji
    2. Silver Ginza Bairin
    3. Bronze Nana Ai Katsu

    Best Thai

    1. Gold Thai Issan Cuisine

    Best Mexican

    1. Gold Da Seafood Cartel

    Best Mediterranean

    1. Finalist Botanico Waikīkī

    Old-School Restaurant We Love

    1. Winner Anna Miller's

    Restaurateur of the Year

    1. Winner Chancelor Kim and Dong Dang (Chao Restaurant Group)

    Source: Honolulu Magazine. Some categories are not shown here.
