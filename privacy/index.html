    Privacy and cookie policy

    Last updated October 11, 2026.

    Restaurant Honolulu (restauranthonolulu.com) is published by Eye To Ad Media. This page explains what information is collected when you use the site and the choices you have.

    Advertising and cookies

    This site shows ads served by Google AdSense. Google and other third-party vendors use cookies to serve ads based on your previous visits to this website and other websites. Google’s use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this site and other sites on the internet.

    • You can opt out of personalized advertising from Google in Google Ads Settings.
    • You can opt out of some third-party vendors’ use of cookies for personalized advertising at www.aboutads.info.
    • Google explains how it uses information from sites that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

    If you opt out, you will still see ads, but they will not be personalized. You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings.

    Forms

    When you send a correction, claim a listing or ask about advertising, the details you type (such as your name, email and message) are delivered to our inbox through the FormSubmit service so we can reply. We use them only to answer you and to update listings.

    Your location

    The near-my-hotel finder and the map can use your device’s location if you press “Use my location” and allow it. The distance calculation happens in your browser. Your location is not sent to us or stored.

    Maps

    The map loads only when you press “Show map”. It then requests map tiles from OpenStreetMap’s servers, which receive your IP address as part of a normal web request. See the OpenStreetMap Foundation privacy policy.

    Links to other sites

    Restaurant pages link to restaurants’ own websites, social accounts and map listings. Those sites have their own privacy policies.

    Contact

    Questions about this policy? Use the contact form.
