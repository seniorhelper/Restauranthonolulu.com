    Eating around Oʻahu

    Outside town, by region. For Honolulu itself start with the directory.

    North Shore

    Haleʻiwa, Kahuku and Pūpūkea — about an hour from Waikīkī.

    North Shore$

    Matsumoto Shave Ice

    Shave ice & dessert

    Haleʻiwa shave ice shop founded in 1951; marked 75 years in business in 2026.

    North Shore$$

    Kahuku Farms Café

    Farm-to-table · Coffee

    Café on a working Kahuku family farm; farm-to-table plates and smoothies, closed Tuesdays and Wednesdays.

    North Shore$$

    Pupukea Grill

    Food trucks · Poke · Vegan & vegetarian

    Sister-run food truck across from Sharks Cove since 2008; poke bowls, plates and vegan tacos.

    North Shore$$$

    Haleiwa Joe's

    Seafood · Steakhouses · American

    Harbor-side Haleʻiwa seafood and steak house since 1998; known for coconut shrimp and prime rib. No reservations.

    North Shore$$

    Giovanni's Shrimp Truck

    Food trucks · Seafood

    Graffiti-covered Kahuku truck serving garlic shrimp scampi plates; lines peak Dec-Mar.

    North Shore$

    Kono's

    Breakfast & brunch · American

    Haleʻiwa counter known for 12-hour kālua pork and Breakfast Bomber burritos.

    Windward side

    Kailua, Kāneʻohe and Waimānalo — 25–40 minutes over the Pali or Likelike.

    Kāneʻohe$$

    Waiāhole Poi Factory

    Hawaiian food

    Hawaiian food stand in a historic Waiāhole Valley poi factory building; second counter at Windward Mall.

    Hale ʻAina 2026 Bronze
    Kailua$$$

    Buzz's Original Steak House

    Steakhouses · Seafood

    Kailua steak house across from Kailua Beach Park, little changed in decades; reserve for dinner.

    Kailua$$$

    Faria

    Fusion

    Chef Kawehi Haug's "Pacifika Portuguese" restaurant in Kailua; Oʻahu's first Portuguese restaurant.

    Kailua$$

    Moke's Bread & Breakfast

    Breakfast & brunch

    Kailua breakfast spot known for lilikoʻi pancakes and handmade corned beef hash; closed Tuesdays, fills early.

    Kāneʻohe$

    Fresh Catch Kāneʻohe

    Poke · Seafood

    Kāneʻohe poke shop and fish market, the larger of Fresh Catch's two locations.

    East Honolulu

    Hawaiʻi Kai, on the way to Hanauma Bay.

    Hawaiʻi Kai$$

    Kona Brewing Hawaiʻi Kai

    Breweries · Bars & pubs · Pizza

    Kona Brewing pub on the Koko Marina docks with lanai seating, trivia and live music nights.

    Happy hour
    Hawaiʻi Kai$$

    Moena Café

    Breakfast & brunch

    Koko Marina brunch café popular after Koko Head hikes; weekend lines after 8:30am.

    Central Oʻahu & Pearl Harbor

    ʻAiea, Pearl City, Wahiawā and Mililani.

    ʻAiea & Pearl City$$

    Anna Miller's

    American · Breakfast & brunch

    1970s-era Pearlridge-area coffee shop and pie house in ʻAiea; Honolulu Magazine's 2026 old-school restaurant pick.

    Hale ʻAina 2026
    ʻAiea & Pearl City$

    Forty Niner Restaurant

    Plate lunch & local food · Udon & noodles

    Old-time ʻAiea diner on the Hawaiʻi historic register, best known for inexpensive saimin; short drive from Pearl Harbor.

    West Oʻahu

    Waipahu, Kapolei and Ko Olina.

    Waipahu$$

    Highway Inn Waipahu

    Hawaiian food · Plate lunch & local food

    Original Highway Inn, a Waipahu Hawaiian-food restaurant dating to 1947; relocated within Waipahu.

    Hale ʻAina 2026 Silver
    Waipahu$$

    Elena's Home of Finest Filipino Foods

    Filipino

    Waipahu Filipino restaurant founded 1974 by the Butuyan family; known for the pork adobo fried rice omelet.

    Kapolei & Ko Olina$$$

    Monkeypod Kitchen Ko Olina

    American · Pizza · Bars & pubs

    Peter Merriman's Monkeypod Kitchen at Ko Olina Station; lunch and dinner daily.

    Open late
