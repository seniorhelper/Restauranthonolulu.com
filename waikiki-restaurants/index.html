Waikiki

Waikiki restaurants that earn the walk from your hotel

Waikiki restaurants worth your time — beachfront dinner, late breakfast, sushi, and the spots that are actually good.

Waikiki restaurants get mocked by people who ate badly here. Fair. The strip rewards laziness. It also holds some of the most cinematic tables in the Pacific — if you treat the neighborhood like a set of jobs, not a single dining scene.

Three jobs a Waikiki restaurant can do

Breakfast before the beach

You want coffee, fruit that tastes like it grew here, and enough food that you don't raid a $9 muffin at 10:30. Go early. See best breakfast in Waikiki.

Sunset as the main course

House Without a Key is the cultural reference: live hula, Diamond Head, a drink, a cake. Pick beach dining on purpose — not by accident.

A real dinner

Some hotel kitchens are excellent. Many are not. Our dinner in Waikiki page is the practical version.

A useful test: would you eat here if it were two blocks inland with no ocean? If the answer is no, you are buying geography. That's allowed.

What “good restaurants in Waikiki” actually means

Search that phrase and you will get a carousel of names you already saw on the airplane magazine. Stay on Kalakaua for convenience. Ride to Kakaako or Ala Moana when you want a neighborhood that isn't dressed as a resort.

What to order on the strip

  • Poke that still tastes like fish — poke in Honolulu
  • A sunset drink with food you won't regret — happy hour
  • One plate-lunch day so you don't go broke on the view
  • One dressed-up night — fine dining
