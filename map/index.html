    Using the map

    The list comes first so you can scan names quickly on a phone. Press Show map to load the interactive map; each pin opens a short card with a link to the restaurant page. The cuisine and area filters apply to both the pins and the list. Sort by distance from me asks your phone for its location, then orders the list nearest first. Your location never leaves your device.

    Most pins cluster in Waikīkī, Kakaʻako, Chinatown and Kaimukī, the areas with the densest dining. North Shore, Kailua and west-side pins are useful for planning a day trip around a meal. Looking for something specific? The meal planner suggests five places from your craving and budget, and the near-my-hotel finder lists what is walkable from your Waikīkī hotel.

    Questions

    Why does the map only load when I press the button?

    The map tiles come from OpenStreetMap’s volunteer-run servers. Loading them only when you ask keeps this page fast and respects their usage policy.

    How accurate are the pins?

    Pins are approximate, to roughly 100 metres, and their original source was not recorded. Use the Directions link on each restaurant page for exact navigation.

    Why are some restaurants not on the map?

    We only pin places with coordinates. The ones without are listed below the map as “location not mapped”.
