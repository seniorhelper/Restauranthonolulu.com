    Helena's Hawaiian Food

    Hawaiian food

    Kalihi Hawaiian-food counter since 1946, known for pipikaula; James Beard America's Classic and 2026 Hale ʻAina Gold.

    Waipahu$$

    Highway Inn Waipahu

    Hawaiian food · Plate lunch & local food

    Original Highway Inn, a Waipahu Hawaiian-food restaurant dating to 1947; relocated within Waipahu.

    Hale ʻAina 2026 Silver
    ʻAiea & Pearl City$$

    Anna Miller's

    American · Breakfast & brunch

    1970s-era Pearlridge-area coffee shop and pie house in ʻAiea; Honolulu Magazine's 2026 old-school restaurant pick.

    Hale ʻAina 2026
    North Shore$

    Matsumoto Shave Ice

    Shave ice & dessert

    Haleʻiwa shave ice shop founded in 1951; marked 75 years in business in 2026.

    Waipahu$$

    Elena's Home of Finest Filipino Foods

    Filipino

    Waipahu Filipino restaurant founded 1974 by the Butuyan family; known for the pork adobo fried rice omelet.

    Kapahulu$

    Leonard's Bakery

    Bakeries & malasadas · Shave ice & dessert

    Kapahulu Portuguese bakery making malasadas since 1952; plain sugar and filled malasada puffs.

    Liliha$

    Liliha Bakery Kuakini

    Bakeries & malasadas · Breakfast & brunch · American

    Original 1950 Liliha Bakery with its old-school counter diner; known for coco puffs.

    Kapahulu$

    Rainbow Drive-In Kapahulu

    Plate lunch & local food

    Original Rainbow Drive-In plate-lunch stand, open since 1961 on Kanaina Ave near Kapahulu; known for mix plates and loco moco.

    North Shore$$

    Pupukea Grill

    Food trucks · Poke · Vegan & vegetarian

    Sister-run food truck across from Sharks Cove since 2008; poke bowls, plates and vegan tacos.
