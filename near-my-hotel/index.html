    How the finder works

    Waikīkī is compact: the beachfront from the Halekulani to the Kapahulu end is under two miles, and most hotels sit within a few blocks of Kalākaua or Kūhiō Avenue. That makes “what can I walk to?” the most useful question after a long flight or a beach day. Pick the landmark nearest your room, set how far you are willing to walk, and the list re-sorts instantly, nearest first, with an approximate walking time.

    Waikīkī starting points use the location of a listed restaurant inside that hotel or shopping center, for example the Halekulani point is where House Without a Key sits. That keeps every point tied to a record on this site. Ala Moana Center, the airport, the cruise pier and Pearl Harbor are included for arrival days and excursions.

    For a sit-down dinner, filter by cuisine and then open the restaurant page to check hours and whether it takes reservations. For a quick bite, try a radius of ¼ mile and look for $ places. Walk-in breakfast and ramen counters cluster along Kalākaua and Kūhiō avenues.

    Questions

    How are walking times worked out?

    We measure the straight-line distance from your starting point to each restaurant and assume about 80 metres (260 feet) a minute. Real routes are longer, so treat the minutes as a minimum.

    My hotel is not on the list. What should I do?

    Pick the closest landmark, or press “Use my location” on your phone. Hilton Hawaiian Village and the Hawaiʻi Convention Center are not listed yet because we have not verified their coordinates.

    Does the finder store my location?

    No. If you allow location access, the distance calculation happens in your browser and nothing is sent to us.

    Why are some restaurants missing?

    Only places with coordinates can be measured. A few listings do not have coordinates yet; you can still find them in the directory.
