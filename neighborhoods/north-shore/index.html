    Harbor-side Haleʻiwa seafood and steak house since 1998; known for coconut shrimp and prime rib. No reservations.

    Giovanni's Shrimp Truck

    Food trucks · Seafood

    Graffiti-covered Kahuku truck serving garlic shrimp scampi plates; lines peak Dec-Mar.

    Kono's

    Breakfast & brunch · American

    Haleʻiwa counter known for 12-hour kālua pork and Breakfast Bomber burritos.

    Ted's Bakery

    Bakeries & malasadas

    Family-run bakery near Sunset Beach on the North Shore, best known for chocolate haupia pie. It stopped wholesale distribution in 2022 and sells its pies and cakes at the shop.

    Good to know

    • Shrimp trucks are cash-friendly but most take cards now; lines peak noon–2 pm.
    • Haleʻiwa traffic is heaviest on winter weekends during big surf.
