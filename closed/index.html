    Honolulu restaurant closures, 2026

    Restaurants that have closed, with dates and what happened. Each page suggests open alternatives nearby.

    Closed in 2026

    • Red Lobster Waikīkī · Waikīkī · June 21, 2026
      Closed June 21, 2026, ending Red Lobster's 32-year presence in Hawaiʻi; corporate cited site-specific operating conditions and lease terms.
    • Smith's Union Bar · Chinatown · June 1, 2026
      Hawaiʻi's oldest bar closed June 1, 2026 after the landlord declined to renew its lease; closure announced December 2025.
    • Mariposa · Ala Moana · May 5, 2026
      Ended service May 5, 2026, ahead of Neiman Marcus Ala Moana's permanent closure in mid-May 2026.
    • Café Miro · Kaimukī · May 2026
      Waiʻalae Avenue restaurant closed in May 2026; Miro Kaimukī – The Counter remains open.
    • Hot Pot Heaven (McCully) · McCully · May 2026
      McCully branch closed in May 2026; Kailua and Kapolei locations remain open.
    • Mahaloha Burger (Kailua) · Kailua · May 2026
      Kailua branch closed in May 2026; Waikīkī and Ala Moana Center locations remain open.
    • Manichi Ramen · Ala Moana · May 2026
      Ala Moana shop closed in May 2026.
    • Morio's Sushi Bistro · Makiki · May 2026
      Makiki sushi bar closed in May 2026.
    • Tchin Tchin! Bar · Chinatown · May 2026 · temporarily closed
      Closed in May 2026 after 11 years; bought by The Royal Leaf owner Ricardo Smith, with a reopening as 'Tchin Tchin Bar by The Royal Leaf' targeted for Sept. 4, 2026 (not yet confirmed).
    • Fort Ruger Market · Diamond Head · April 2026
      Closed in April 2026; owner said Korean convenience chain CU Hawaii is taking over the space.
    • Monarch Poke · ʻAiea & Pearl City · April 2026
      ʻAiea shop closed in April 2026.
    • Nancy's Kitchen · Waipahu · April 2026
      Waipahu and Waipiʻo locations closed in April 2026.
    • Side Street Inn (Kapahulu) · Kapahulu · April 2026
      Closed after the first week of April 2026 after nearly 20 years and relocated to 280 Beach Walk in Waikīkī; Hopaka St and ʻEwa Beach locations remain open.
    • Tamashiro Market · Kalihi · April 2026
      Closed in April 2026 after about 80 years in Kalihi.
    • Chicken & Brisket · ʻAiea & Pearl City · March 2026
      Closed in March 2026.
    • House of Wong · Kapahulu · March 2026
      Closed in March 2026.
    • ʻAi Love Nalo · Waimānalo · March 2026
      Closed in early 2026 (listed under February/March).
    • Miyoshi Ramen · Kailua · March 2026
      Kailua shop closed in March 2026.
    • Hoku's · Kāhala · February 20, 2026
      Final service Feb. 20, 2026; The Kahala Hotel & Resort replaced it with a new Alan Wong's.
    • JINYA Ramen Bar (Ward Village) · Kakaʻako · February 9, 2026
      Closed Feb. 9, 2026 per a state WARN notice laying off 86 workers; Robata Jinya in the same complex closed too.
    • Jesse's Coffee Shop & Restaurant · Kalihi · February 2026
      Closed in February 2026.
    • Kulu Kulu (Royal Hawaiian Center) · Waikīkī · February 2026
      Royal Hawaiian Center shop closed in February 2026; the Hobron Lane location remains open.
    • Mo-Joe's Sports Bar & Grill · Kāneʻohe · February 2026
      Closed in February 2026.
    • Ramen Bario (Ala Moana) · Ala Moana · February 2026
      Ala Moana branch closed in February 2026; the Stix Asia location in Waikīkī remains open.
    • Robata Jinya (Ward Village) · Kakaʻako · February 2026
      Closed in February 2026 alongside JINYA Ramen Bar at Ward Village.
    • Camado Ramen Tavern · Waikīkī · January 11, 2026
      Closed at its Lewers Street location on Jan. 11, 2026, per the restaurant's website.
    • Aloha Alfajores · Wahiawā · January 2026
      Wahiawā shop closed in January 2026.
    • Coffee Gallery · North Shore · January 2026
      Closed in January 2026.
    • Leila · Kaimukī · January 2026
      Closed in January 2026; it had switched from Moroccan to an Italian/Mediterranean prix fixe.

    Closed in 2025

    • Ethel's Grill · Kalihi · November 27, 2025
      Closed Nov. 27, 2025, the day before Thanksgiving, after 46 years; co-owner cited rising costs and pressures common to local eateries.
    • Sorabol · Ala Moana · March 7, 2025
      Closed March 7, 2025 after about 40 years; owner retired citing rising costs and planned Pagoda Hotel renovations.
    • KuruKuru Sushi (ʻAiea) · ʻAiea & Pearl City · February 2025
      Closed in February 2025 after 22 years in ʻAiea.

    Earlier closures

    • Encore Saloon · Chinatown · March 29, 2024
      Closed after almost eight years in Chinatown; owner cited staffing, food and labor costs.
    • 12th Ave Grill · Kaimukī · January 16, 2022
      Closed in January 2022 after 18 years in Kaimukī.
    • Alan Wong's (King Street) · Mōʻiliʻili · November 2020
      Flagship closed permanently in November 2020 amid pandemic fallout after 25 years; Alan Wong's later reopened at The Kahala Hotel & Resort in Hoku's former space.
    • ʻOno Hawaiian Foods · Kapahulu · August 26, 2017
      Closed after 57 years; space reopened as Da Ono Hawaiian Food.
    • Hong Yeon · Mōʻiliʻili
      Listed as closed on Yelp (Sept. 2026) roughly a year after its Sept. 10, 2025 opening; no closure date or announcement found.
    • Little Village Noodle House · Chinatown
      Closed for good after 22 years (c. 2023); owner cited staffing shortages.
    • Shirokiya Japan Village Walk · Ala Moana
      Ala Moana Center food hall closed in 2020 at the start of the pandemic; equipment auctioned in March 2023.
    • Sushi ii · Ala Moana · temporarily closed
      Listed as closed on Yelp (Sept. 2026) and 'temporarily closed' on AutoReserve; no dated closure announcement found.
    • Sweet Home Waimānalo · Waimānalo
      Listed as permanently closed; its 41-1025 Kalanianaʻole Hwy address is now associated with ʻAi Love Nalo.
