    Why Oʻahu is worth showing up for

    Oʻahu received 5.79 million visitors by air in 2024, by far the most of any Hawaiian island, according to the state’s Data Book. In the first ten months of 2025 visitors spent $7.88 billion on the island, up 4.9%, even as arrivals dipped 1.2%.

    Most of them don’t know a single restaurant name when they land. The places that show a photo, current hours and a menu they can actually read are the ones that end up on the short list.

    Sources: DBEDT Data Book 2024, table 7.07 · DBEDT October 2025 visitor statistics. Checked October 26, 2026.

    Questions owners ask

    Is a basic listing really free?

    Yes. Any open restaurant on Oʻahu can claim its listing on Restaurant Honolulu at no cost and correct hours, phone, address and links. We confirm you are the owner or manager before anything changes. Featured and Menu Pro are optional, and buying one never decides whether you stay listed.

    What is the difference between Featured and Menu Pro?

    Featured ($29 a month) gives you a labeled top spot on the neighborhood and cuisine pages that match your restaurant, with a photo, a short pitch and your link. Menu Pro ($49 a month or $490 a year) includes that same Featured placement plus a hosted menu page Google can read, a full menu update every quarter and NFC table tags, all done for you.

    How is a paid spot labeled?

    Every paid placement carries a visible Sponsored or Featured label, and the link to your website is marked rel="sponsored" as search engines ask. Paid spots never change the order of our Best of lists, the Hale ʻAina award listings or the directory itself.

    Will it guarantee more customers?

    No. We promise the work: a labeled top placement with your photo and link, and with Menu Pro a menu that search engines and AI assistants can read and that we keep current. Whether a visitor picks you still comes down to your food, reviews and service.

    How fast does it go live?

    Usually within 48 hours of receiving your photo, pitch and link. Menu Pro menu pages usually go live within 48 hours of getting your menu, sent as photos, a PDF or a document. We verify you run the restaurant first.

    How is this different from the plans on the advertise page?

    The Verified, Featured and Premium plans on the advertise page are set up by invoice after a quick conversation. The plans on this page can be bought instantly online. If you want Premium extras like a photo gallery, a deals box or Pop-up Domination, use the advertise page and we will put together the right package.

    Can I cancel?

    Yes. Monthly plans can be cancelled anytime online and stay live through the month you have paid for. Menu Pro yearly is one payment of $490 for twelve months, two months free compared with monthly.
