    Kahuku Farms Café

    Farm-to-table · Coffee

    Café on a working Kahuku family farm; farm-to-table plates and smoothies, closed Tuesdays and Wednesdays.

    Waikīkī—

    Hala Tree Café Waikīkī

    Coffee · Bakeries & malasadas

    Flagship café of the Hala Tree Kona coffee farm, moved from Kaʻaʻawa to The Lilia Waikīkī in June 2026. It pours the farm’s own 100% Kona coffee and has an in-house bakery; the entrance is on Kānekapōlei Street.
